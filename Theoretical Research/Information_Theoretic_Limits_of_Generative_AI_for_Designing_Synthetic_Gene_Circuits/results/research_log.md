## Requested topic

Information-theoretic limits of generative AI for designing synthetic gene circuits.

## Selected formulation

**Two Information Channels, One Design Limit: An Information-Theoretic Framework for Generative AI in Synthetic Gene-Circuit Design.** A target-label proposal channel followed by a context-conditioned realization channel. The mathematical core consists of the data-processing inequality and the composition of independent K-ary symmetric channels. Synthetic examples make consequences calculable without representing the work as an empirical biological result.

## Assumptions

- Desired function can be represented by one of K discrete classes for the illustrative analysis.
- Conditional Markov dependence `T -> G -> Y` is assumed after conditioning on context.
- Proposal and realization errors are independent and symmetric for the closed-form composition example.
- The example values `K=4`, `e_g=0.10`, and `e_b=0.15` are hypothetical.
- The normalized context-shift response and binomial finite-sample model are synthetic sensitivity illustrations.
