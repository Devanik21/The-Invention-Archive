# Publication design brief

- **Audience/format:** Mathematical biology, evolutionary dynamics, and formal control readers; an independent theoretical/synthetic preprint, with no claim of journal submission or peer review.
- **Page architecture:** A4, 0.82-inch margins, polished title/abstract page, two-page contents due to a 23-section/appendix structure, 20 substantive main-text pages, proof/reproducibility appendices, references.
- **Typography:** Latin Modern with T1 encoding and microtype; 11 pt body; consistent mathematical fonts; 22 pt header height; body line spacing 1.12; numbered equations and theorem/proposition environments.
- **Running elements:** Short left running title “HODGE OBSTRUCTION”; right side “PREPRINT • 25 OCT 2026”; centered page number.
- **Palette:** restrained ink/navy headings, teal links/subheadings, coral for caveats; plot colors are consistent and used only to distinguish declared model categories.
- **Headings/callouts:** navy section headings, teal subsection headings, thin teal header rule, a concise key-conclusion box and a coral scope warning on the first page.
- **Equations:** Introduce symbols in prose; number important equations; use aligned/labelled displays; state assumptions beside propositions and keep proof details in appendix.
- **Tables:** Booktabs rules without vertical grid lines; five substantive content tables covering epistemic classification, literature, model assumptions, synthetic results, and falsification.
- **Figures:** Twelve numbered, code-generated assets; 8 analytical plots, 2 graph/network views, 2 method/overview flows; vector PDF assets embedded in the paper and PNG previews retained with source/CSV outputs.
- **Captions/references:** Numbered captions placed consistently; figures and tables are cited/discussed in the text; natbib `plainnat` bibliography with DOI links.
- **Whitespace:** Preserve readable leading and natural figure placement; avoid ornamental blank pages. Long tables may wrap at ordinary scale, but source widths were revised after first visual pass.
- **Acceptance principle:** Design features support scientific interpretation; page and figure counts are necessary floors, not substitutes for readable content or evidence.
