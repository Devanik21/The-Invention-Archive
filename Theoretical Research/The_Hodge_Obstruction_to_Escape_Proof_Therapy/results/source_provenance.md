# Source provenance and data availability

No public empirical dataset was downloaded or included in the numerical benchmark. Fitness coefficients, genotype states, transition supports, and measurement-noise examples are generated by `verify_model.py` from transparent synthetic model assumptions. The CSVs in this directory are outputs of that script. Primary literature metadata and DOI links are listed in `literature_audit.csv` and `paper.bib`.

This paper does not reproduce the experimental results of any cited source. Any future empirical validation must acquire and version the original data, check licenses and preprocessing, rebuild the genotype graph and cell complex, model assay uncertainty, use held-out transition tests, and compare against established fitness-only, sequence-optimization, and reinforcement-learning baselines. No experimental protocol or treatment recommendation is provided.
