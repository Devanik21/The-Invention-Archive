                                                   Abstract
         Drug-sequence design seeks to steer evolving pathogen populations away from states that
     support multi-drug resistance. A tempting topological hypothesis is that a Hodge decomposition
     of drug-specific collateral-effect fields reveals a curl or harmonic obstruction to a schedule that
     remains safe indefinitely. This paper subjects that hypothesis to a structural test before using
     it as a design principle. If, for each drug, an edge effect is defined as the difference of a single
     scalar fitness potential at the two endpoint genotypes, then the edge field is exact: its circulation
     around every closed path is zero by telescoping, and its square-face curl vanishes identically. On
     the complete cubical complex, the first cohomology also vanishes. Therefore, nonzero curl cannot
     be a structural property of a consistent static scalar fitness landscape; it instead signals noisy or
     incomplete measurements, a mismatched field definition, or genuinely state- and history-dependent
     dynamics. Hodge analysis remains useful as a data-consistency diagnostic, but it does not by itself
     decide whether escape-proof therapy exists. We then formulate escape avoidance as a finite-state
     robust safety game and prove that its greatest fixed point is the exact viability kernel for a specified
     successor relation. A finite-horizon probabilistic dynamic program and tabular Q-learning baseline
     are compared on a reproducible, four-locus, three-landscape synthetic benchmark. The synthetic
     result is intentionally modest: a robustly safe subset exists for the declared toy rules, while one-step
     greedy and round-robin policies enter the synthetic failure set frequently in seeded simulations.
     These numbers are not antimicrobial measurements and support no clinical recommendation. The
     paper’s contribution is a falsifiable separation principle: audit the measured edge field with Hodge
     tools, but establish safety with reachability and viability analysis under explicit mutation and
     observation assumptions.

Keywords: antimicrobial resistance; evolutionary game theory; combinatorial Hodge decomposition;
viability kernel; partial observation; drug sequencing; synthetic benchmark; reinforcement learning.

  Core conclusion. A consistent scalar fitness landscape produces an exact edge field with zero
  cycle circulation. A therapy schedule is escape-proof only relative to a specified transition model,
  failure set, observation model, and adversary—not because a Hodge component happens to be
  nonzero.

1 Introduction: from drug sequences to safety guarantees
Antimicrobial resistance is an evolutionary control problem with an asymmetric objective. A sequence
may temporarily suppress growth yet leave the population in a genetic background from which cross-
resistance is easier to reach. Empirical and model-based work has therefore investigated sequential
drug use as a way to steer evolutionary trajectories, rather than treating each drug as an isolated
intervention. Studies of collateral sensitivity show both the potential of sequencing and its dependence
on evolutionary repeatability: the same first drug may lead different replicates to different genetic
endpoints, changing their response to a later drug [Nichol et al., 2019, Maltas and Wood, 2019,
Ardell and Kryazhimskiy, 2021]. A recent reinforcement-learning study demonstrates that treatment
cycling policies can be learned over empirically measured landscapes in silico despite noisy or delayed
observations [Weaver et al., 2024]. These are important precedents, not evidence that a universal
escape-proof schedule exists.
   The question here is more stringent than minimizing average growth. Let genotype be a vertex of a
finite mutation graph, let a drug be an action, and let the transition model describe which mutations
can follow that action. Mark a subset of vertices as failure states—for example, genotypes above
declared resistance thresholds for multiple drugs. Does there exist a policy that keeps the trajectory
outside that set for all future time, despite allowed mutation outcomes and incomplete information?
This is a safety-game question. Its answer depends on the transition relation, the failure set, and what
the controller can observe.
   The proposed title invokes a Hodge obstruction because topological summaries of edge flows can
distinguish gradient, local-circulation, and harmonic components. Combinatorial Hodge methods have
a well-developed mathematical foundation and have been applied to biological structures and data
analysis [Jiang et al., 2011, Wei et al., 2022]. The critical issue is whether the particular edge field in
drug-sequence design is actually a non-conservative flow. When it is constructed from differences of a
scalar fitness landscape, the answer is no: the field is a discrete gradient by definition. The paper
makes that logical constraint explicit and redirects topology from a purported cause of therapeutic
impossibility to an audit of data coherence.
   The deliverable is a set of definitions, two finite-state results, and a small executable benchmark.
The structural result rules out one interpretation of the conjecture under its own assumptions. The
safety-game theorem supplies the correct object for an escape guarantee: the greatest controlled-
invariant subset of the safe region. A finite-horizon stochastic counterpart quantifies risk when
transitions are not treated adversarially. The synthetic example demonstrates why a static landscape
summary and a control guarantee answer different questions.


2 Research posture and claim classification
We treat the initial Hodge-obstruction conjecture as a hypothesis to be falsified, not a desired
conclusion. The manuscript distinguishes exact mathematics from modeled dynamics and synthetic
outputs. An algebraic identity on a hypercube is not a claim that a pathogen follows a hypercube in
full biological detail. A viability result is conditional on the transition relation used to compute it.
The benchmark is deliberately small enough that exact dynamic programming and robust fixed-point
computation can be independently checked.
  The contribution has three parts. First, it provides a no-go statement: when every drug-specific
edge field is the exact difference of a scalar node potential, its closed-loop circulation must vanish.
Second, it states a finite-state safety-game characterization of infinite-horizon escape avoidance and
makes the partial-observation extension explicit. Third, it provides a reproducible toy benchmark

3 Related work and the actual gap
3.1 Evolution on measured fitness landscapes
Mira and colleagues measured adaptive landscapes for TEM β-lactamase genotypes across multiple
antibiotics and concentration conditions, supporting combinatorially complete analyses of a small


                                                     5
HODGE OBSTRUCTION                                                           PREPRINT • 25 OCT 2026



mutational system [Mira et al., 2015]. Nichol et al. used landscape structure to demonstrate that
collateral sensitivity can be contingent on the trajectory generated under the first treatment, rather
than being a deterministic mapping from one drug to the next [Nichol et al., 2019]. Maltas and
Wood analyzed diverse collateral-sensitivity profiles and optimal sequencing strategies, including
laboratory population experiments [Maltas and Wood, 2019]. Ardell and Kryazhimskiy developed a
population-genetic theory for collateral resistance and sensitivity [Ardell and Kryazhimskiy, 2021].
Together these studies motivate trajectory-level planning and warn against assuming all replicates
follow a single path.
   A measured finite landscape does not automatically define an escape-proof policy. It supplies
phenotype or fitness values for genotypes under specified conditions; it does not by itself determine
a universally correct mutation kernel. Mutation rates, fixation probabilities, population size, drug
concentration, spatial structure, and observation noise may change which transitions are feasible
or likely. Mira et al. specifically showed that adaptive landscape structure depends on antibiotic
concentration, reinforcing the need to state environmental conditions rather than treat fitness values
as immutable properties [Mira et al., 2015].

3.2 Optimal control and reinforcement learning
Nichol et al. modelled sequential therapy as a biased random walk on fitness landscapes and analyzed
how drug order changes the emergence of resistance [Nichol et al., 2015]. Weaver et al. used empirically
parameterized landscapes and reinforcement learning to learn drug cycling policies under partial, noisy,
or delayed information; they compared learned strategies with exact optimization and simpler cycling
policies [Weaver et al., 2024]. Therefore, the broad idea of using RL to optimize drug sequences is not
new and is not claimed as this paper’s novelty. Here, tabular Q-learning is a transparent baseline; it
is not a clinical agent and does not certify worst-case safety.

3.3 Hodge decomposition and fitness data
Combinatorial Hodge theory decomposes edge flows into gradient-like, curl-like, and harmonic compo-
nents when an appropriate simplicial or cubical complex is specified. Jiang et al. developed HodgeRank
for ranking from pairwise comparisons, where cyclic inconsistency is a meaningful property of observed
comparisons [Jiang et al., 2011]. Wei et al. applied Hodge tools to biomolecular structure and biological
data represented by simplicial complexes [Wei et al., 2022]. These works support the mathematical
legitimacy of a Hodge audit; they do not imply that every biological edge field is non-conservative or
that its Hodge components determine policy viability.

3.4 The defensible gap
The narrow gap addressed here is not the general use of Hodge decomposition in biology, nor drug
scheduling via reinforcement learning. It is the need to separate (i) consistency diagnostics for
measured edge effects from (ii) robust reachability guarantees under an explicit drug-controlled
transition relation. We could not establish absolute novelty from targeted searches, and adjacent work
is extensive. The specific contribution is a no-go correction to the proposed interpretation plus a
reproducible safety-game formulation. Table 2 contrasts research roles.

4 Genotype complex, landscapes, and orientation
Let G = (V, E) be a finite, connected mutation graph. A vertex x ∈ V represents a genotype or
coarse-grained genetic state; an undirected edge connects genotypes one allowed mutation apart. For
a four-locus binary example, V = {0, 1}4 has 16 vertices and 32 edges. We consider m candidate
drugs/actions indexed by a ∈ A = {1, . . . , m}. Under fixed assay conditions, drug a is represented by
a scalar landscape fa : V → R.
  Orient each edge arbitrarily. For oriented edge e = (u, v), define the drug-specific edge cochain

                                            ωa (e) = fa (v) − fa (u).                                           (1)

Reversing orientation changes the sign. This is the discrete analogue of a gradient field. It represents
a fitness difference only when both endpoint values are measurements of the same landscape under
the same environmental and assay definition. It is not the same object as a mutation probability,
transition log-odds, or policy-dependent average velocity.
   Epistasis does not invalidate Eq. (1). Epistasis changes the values of fa on vertices, but the
differences still telescope as long as a single-valued landscape is used. Multiple drugs may have
different and opposing gradients; each ωa remains exact separately. Switching environments creates a
nonautonomous controlled process, but it does not make each drug’s static edge field non-exact.

6 Hodge decomposition: what it can and cannot tell us
For a finite oriented 2-complex, a 1-cochain w ∈ R|E| may be decomposed orthogonally, under the
Euclidean inner product, into exact, coexact, and harmonic components:

                           w = B0 f + B2⊤ ϕ + h,                      h ∈ ker(B0⊤ ) ∩ ker(B2 ).                           (3)

The exact part captures edge differences induced by node scores; the coexact part captures local
circulation represented by oriented 2-faces; and the harmonic part captures cycle structure not explained
by those local faces. Precise operator naming depends on whether boundary or coboundary operators
are used and how the complex is oriented, but the finite-dimensional orthogonal decomposition is
standard [Jiang et al., 2011, Wei et al., 2022].

7 What apparent curl might mean
An observed circulation statistic remains worth reporting when carefully defined. We separate four
cases. (i) If edge data were obtained by subtracting common, noise-free node scores, nonzero circulation
indicates implementation or orientation error. (ii) If each edge effect was independently estimated
with uncertainty, nonzero circulation can arise from measurement noise; the inference should quantify
its scale. (iii) If the measured variable is not a fitness difference—such as a directional transition
log-odds—it may legitimately be non-conservative, but its interpretation differs from a scalar fitness
gradient. (iv) If fitness depends on hidden state, time, drug history, concentration, population
composition, or spatial environment, a single static potential may be inadequate. The model then
needs those state variables or must treat the field as an approximation.

   A nonzero curl could become mechanistically meaningful if a model explicitly defines a non-gradient
edge field, for example from cyclic transition preferences or context-dependent dynamical observations.
The empirical question is then whether that field predicts transitions or resistance outcomes beyond
known fitness, mutational, and assay variables. A prospective test would fit the field on part of the data
and assess calibration and predictive value on held-out trajectories. Without such evidence, “hysteresis-
like” remains an analogy. History dependence requires comparing matched current genotypes after
distinct randomized drug histories and observing whether transition distributions differ after controlling
for measured confounders.


8 Escape avoidance as a robust safety game
Define a failure set R ⊆ V and a safe set S = V \ R. For each genotype x and drug a, let
Succ(x, a) ⊆ V be the set of possible one-step successors under the chosen model. A conservative
strong-selection/weak-mutation stylization can include the current genotype and every one-mutation
neighbor that is beneficial under drug a. In another application, it could be a confidence set for
mutation transitions or an adversarial support set obtained from uncertain parameters.
    The controller observes state x, chooses a drug a, and then nature or the evolutionary process
selects any successor in Succ(x, a). The goal is to stay in S for all time. A policy is robustly safe
from x if every possible trajectory starting there avoids R. This is intentionally stronger than a
low average resistance probability. It provides a worst-case guarantee only relative to the declared
successor relation; if that relation omits a biologically possible mutation, the guarantee does not cover
it.

9 Model uncertainty and robust-kernel sensitivity
A controller may know the genotype but not the complete mutation support. Treating a guessed
successor graph as exact can turn a numerical certificate into a false guarantee. Let T be a set of
admissible transition relations, each with successor set ΓT (x, a). The conservative predecessor operator
is
                       PreT (K) = {x ∈ K : ∃a ∈ A(x), ∀T ∈ T, ΓT (x, a) ⊆ K}.                         (4)

The robust kernel is the greatest fixed point obtained by starting at the declared safe set and iterating
this operator. A state survives only if a single action works for every transition relation in the
uncertainty family. This order of quantifiers is stronger than selecting an action separately after
discovering which transition model is true.

Proposition 9.1 (Monotonicity under transition uncertainty). Suppose T1 ⊆ T2 and both uncertainty
families use the same state space, action availability, and safe set. Then their robust kernels satisfy
K∞ (T2 ) ⊆ K∞ (T1 ).

Proof. For any candidate K, an action whose successors remain in K for every T ∈ T2 also does so for
every T ∈ T1 , because the latter family contains no additional relation. Thus PreT2 (K) ⊆ PreT1 (K).
Both iterations begin at the same safe set. Induction gives inclusion at every iteration, and taking the
finite fixed-point limits preserves inclusion. □
   This result is elementary but operationally important: enlarging a defensible uncertainty set can
only preserve or shrink the certified region. It cannot improve a worst-case guarantee. Empirical
use should therefore report how the kernel changes when plausible mutation edges, compensatory
transitions, drug-specific observations, or unmeasured genotypes are added. A large kernel under an
artificially sparse graph is not persuasive evidence of robust treatment design.
  For measured landscapes, uncertainty can enter at two different layers. Fitness uncertainty changes
the estimated direction or probability of an edge; transition uncertainty changes which edges are

                                                        12
HODGE OBSTRUCTION                                                            PREPRINT • 25 OCT 2026



possible at all. The first can be represented by intervals or posterior distributions over potential
differences. The second is a support question. A model that assigns tiny probability to an observed
escape route has not eliminated that route from a robust game. In contrast, a finite-horizon expected-
risk objective may legitimately weight it by probability, provided the calibration and loss function are
justified.
   An appropriate diagnostic should distinguish three statuses: certified safe under all declared
transitions, low-risk under a calibrated probabilistic model, and unresolved because the measuremen-
t/transition support is incomplete. Collapsing those into a single “escape-free score” would hide the
principal assumptions on which the conclusion depends.


10 The viability kernel and an exact theorem
For a subset Q ⊆ S, define the controlled robust predecessor

                             Pre(Q) = {x ∈ S : ∃a ∈ A, Succ(x, a) ⊆ Q}.                               (5)

Let K0 = S and recursively set Kn+1 = Pre(Kn ). The sequence is decreasing: a state is removed
whenever every action admits at least one possible successor already excluded. Because V is finite,
the sequence stabilizes in at most |V | strict reductions. Its fixed point is the robust viability kernel.

Theorem 10.1 (Finite-state robust viability characterization). Let V and A be finite and let Succ(x, a)
be nonempty for every state-action pair. The limit K = n≥0 Kn is exactly the set of states from
                                                            T

which a policy can guarantee xt ∈ S for every t ≥ 0 against every successor allowed by Succ. For
every x ∈ K, at least one action witnesses robust invariance. For every x ∈ S \ K, no policy can
guarantee indefinite safety under the stated successor relation.

Proof. Since the sets are finite and decreasing, there is an index N for which KN +1 = KN . Write
this set as K. By the fixed-point definition, for every x ∈ K there exists an action a such that all
successors lie in K. Selecting such an action at every state in K keeps the next state in K; induction
proves safety at all future times.
   Conversely, any state removed at iteration n + 1 is either already unsafe or, for every action, has
at least one successor outside Kn . An adversary can choose such a successor. By induction on the
removal iteration, any state outside the fixed point admits a finite sequence of allowed adversarial
responses that reaches the failure set. Thus no policy guarantees perpetual safety from that state.
This is a finite-state reachability argument; it does not use Hodge decomposition.

   The fixed-point iteration for the synthetic benchmark is shown in Fig. 8. It reports the size of the
candidate safe set after each elimination step, and the final kernel is stored in results/summary.json.
A nonempty kernel means the toy rules admit a robustly safe region. It does not establish that such a
region exists for a real pathogen or that a clinically feasible regimen can keep a population inside it.

14 A role for machine learning without confusing prediction and
   proof
A learned landscape model can estimate fa (x) for unmeasured genotypes from sequence features, but
its role is prediction. It does not, merely by fitting observed fitness, certify that its extrapolations
preserve the true ranking or transition support. For safety analysis, an uncertainty-aware model
should provide a set Fa (x) of plausible fitness values or a confidence bound. A conservative mutation
relation then includes every transition possible under some landscape in the uncertainty set. The
viability kernel computed with that enlarged relation is smaller than or equal to the kernel computed
under a point estimate, because additional possible successors make robust control more difficult.
   RL can be useful where the transition model is large, partially observed, or changes over time.
However, reward optimization is not the same as a hard safety constraint. A Q-learning policy may
obtain good average return while retaining nonzero probability of entering a failure state. A safe-RL
formulation needs a shield or constraint mechanism based on a verified safe set, and the shield itself
depends on the validity of the transition model. The prior Weaver et al. study already establishes
the relevance of RL for treatment-cycle learning under imperfect observation in modeled landscapes
[Weaver et al., 2024]; this paper does not relabel that contribution as new.

14.1 Synthetic Q-learning baseline
The released code trains a tabular Q-learning policy for 9,000 episodes using the same finite genotype-
state simulator and a penalty for entering the synthetic failure set. All episodes use deterministic
random seeds for reproducibility. The learned policy is evaluated in independent 5,000-trajectory
Monte Carlo runs. These choices permit a fair and inspectable software benchmark while remaining
purposefully small. The policy is neither evidence of a general learning advantage nor a deployable
antimicrobial treatment controller; its reward, transition model, and state representation are all
stylized and fully known.


15 Synthetic benchmark design
The benchmark uses a 4-bit genotype hypercube with three scalar fitness landscapes. Landscape
values are generated from a fixed intercept, four additive coefficients, and six pairwise interaction
coefficients for each drug. These coefficients were selected as a transparent toy construction to create
trade-offs and epistasis, not estimated from data. The failure set comprises states whose synthetic
fitness meets the declared threshold under at least two of the three landscapes. In the released run,
the threshold is zero in the dimensionless synthetic scale, yielding a finite nonempty failure set. This
definition is internal to the example and must not be read as a resistance breakpoint.
   For robust safety, a drug-specific successor set contains the current genotype and each one-locus
neighbor with strictly greater synthetic fitness under that drug. The safety game assumes a worst-case
selector chooses any member of this set after the drug is applied. For probabilistic risk, a separate
transition matrix is formed by assigning a weight of one to stasis and a weight proportional to
exp(β∆f ) to each beneficial neighbor, then normalizing. The parameter β = 5 is dimensionless and
chosen only for this benchmark. Using different transition assumptions is deliberate: robust support
and stochastic probabilities should not be conflated.

kernel, finite-horizon risk, figures, and CSV data. It tests orientation consistency, stochastic row

16 Results I: Hodge consistency is a structural identity
The numerical edge differences were generated by taking endpoint differences of the three scalar fitness
arrays. The maximum absolute square circulation for exact fields is at floating-point precision (roughly
10−16 for this run). The check B2 B0 = 0 passes exactly in the constructed incidence matrices. Hodge
projections reconstruct each edge field within numerical tolerance, and the exact components dominate
the energy. These observations match Proposition 5.1; they are not a discovery of a biological property
because the construction enforces the identity.
   When seeded independent noise with standard deviation 0.08 is added to edge values, the root-
mean-square square circulation becomes visibly nonzero. Figure 11 varies the synthetic noise level
and shows the diagnostic responding to corruption. The appropriate interpretation is that cycle sums
can detect disagreement among edge measurements. A rigorous empirical study would compare the
statistic to a null distribution that includes assay error, repeated-measure variance, missingness, and
the procedure used to estimate the edge field. It would also test whether a model allowing context
dependence improves held-out prediction enough to justify its complexity.
   The initial conjecture stated that nonzero curl of drug-specific collateral-effect fields should decide
whether an escape-proof schedule exists and lower-bound the number of drugs required. Under the
scalar-potential interpretation, this cannot be correct as stated: the curl is zero before any policy
is considered. Under an alternative definition of the field, nonzero curl may be meaningful, but its
relationship to a safety guarantee must be established separately. This negative result prevents a
topological analogy from being promoted into a theorem without a matching definition.

18 Results III: threshold, noise, and model sensitivity
The safety answer changes when the failure set changes. A stricter target may classify more states as
unsafe and shrink the viability kernel; a more permissive target may enlarge it. Figure 12 reports
a threshold sweep with the transition relation held fixed. This is not parameter uncertainty in a
resistance threshold; it demonstrates that a formal safety claim cannot be evaluated until the failure
predicate is explicit. In real studies, the target requires phenotype criteria justified by organism, drug,
assay, and clinical context.
   The Hodge diagnostic has a different sensitivity profile. In the synthetic corruption experiment,
independent edge noise tends to increase apparent cycle circulation. If noisy node fitness estimates
are first fitted to a common scalar landscape and edge values are derived from those fitted nodes, the
reconstructed field becomes exact by construction; uncertainty then appears in fitted potentials and
residuals, rather than as independent edge curls. These are different analysis pipelines, and their
results should not be mixed. A defensible empirical report should state whether the edge field was
directly measured, computed from endpoint scores, or estimated jointly with a potential.
   The model does not implement a neural surrogate for unseen genotypes or claim a large-genome
scaling result. Such an extension would be meaningful only if it preserved held-out accuracy and
calibrated uncertainty. An adversarial benchmark should compare an accurate model, an overconfident
extrapolator, and a distribution shift in which mutational effects change outside training support.
The relevant output is the probability that the claimed safe set contains actually unsafe states, not
just prediction RMSE. This becomes important as the safety-game relation is widened to account for
uncertainty.

20 Polyhedral landscapes and alternative baselines
Regular subdivisions and related polyhedral descriptions of fitness landscapes organize epistatic
structure and genotype combinations under selection. These are complementary to the Hodge audit
and safety game. A polyhedral representation summarizes geometry of a fitness function; it does not
by itself answer which mutation transitions are feasible after each treatment or whether every allowed
path can be prevented from entering a failure set. Conversely, the safety game can be solved from an
explicit transition relation without a polyhedral construction. A meaningful comparison would ask
whether polyhedral features predict the shape of viable regions or reduce the state space needed for
exact planning, using the same held-out landscape instances and failure definition.
   One baseline is the one-step greedy policy that chooses the drug with the lowest current synthetic
fitness. It is locally intuitive but ignores long-run accessibility. Another is round-robin cycling; it is
simple and reproducible but does not adapt to current state. The exact dynamic program is an oracle
for its specified stochastic kernel and horizon, while the robust fixed-point computation is an oracle for
the declared adversarial successor relation. Q-learning is an approximate learning baseline. Comparing
them is valid only when objective, information, transition assumptions, initial-state distribution, and
evaluation horizon are aligned. In this paper, the robust game and stochastic DP are reported as
separate questions, not as interchangeable rankings.

21 Toward empirical use: a staged validation protocol
A real-data study would begin with a combinatorially complete landscape or an explicitly justified
partial landscape for a small mutational system. Public TEM β-lactamase work provides a precedent
for a landscape with 16 genotypes across 15 antibiotics, with later studies using those data for sequence
optimization [Mira et al., 2015, Nichol et al., 2015, Weaver et al., 2024]. In a new analysis, the data
version, genotype encoding, assay conditions, and uncertainty estimates must be fixed before evaluating
a Hodge-derived feature. This manuscript did not download or reanalyze those measurements; it uses
them only as established context.
   A conservative validation pipeline would have five stages. First, reconstruct node-level fitness
distributions with replicate uncertainty and retain the exact genotype-environment mapping. Second,
estimate uncertainty on edge contrasts and test cycle sums under a null model in which all edges derive
from common node potentials. Third, define a phenotype-based failure set and state which transitions
remain plausible under confidence bounds. Fourth, compute robust viability and finite-horizon risk
under several defensible transition models, comparing against exact small-system solutions. Fifth,
evaluate prediction and policy performance on held-out trajectories and report how often an allegedly
safe policy violates its constraints under uncertainty expansion.
    No human or animal intervention follows from this theoretical exercise. An eventual wet-lab
investigation would need institutional biosafety review, organism- and assay-specific controls, antimi-
crobial stewardship oversight, and a protocol designed with qualified microbiology experts. Treatment
schedules inferred in silico are not clinical prescriptions. The intended human benefit is indirect:
better-calibrated tools for comparing evolutionary control strategies before costly or risky validation,
if future empirical studies support them.


22 Limitations and threats to validity
The benchmark has deliberate limitations. Its 16 genotypes, three synthetic landscapes, threshold-
defined failure set, and stylized beneficial-mutation kernel are far smaller and simpler than a real
pathogen population. The model contains no recombination, horizontal gene transfer, demographic
bottlenecks, spatial structure, phenotypic heterogeneity, persistence, immune response, pharmacoki-
netics, toxicity, or mixed infections. Its stochastic kernel is not an SSWM model calibrated to a known
organism. The Monte Carlo outcomes demonstrate code behavior, not a biological fact.
   The robust viability theorem is exact but conditional: an omitted successor invalidates the guarantee.
Partial observation makes the problem harder because a belief state must account for all genotypes
compatible with the data, and the number of beliefs can be exponential in genotype count. Hodge
decompositions also depend on the complex selected for analysis. A graph without filled squares
cannot reveal whether every cycle is a boundary; harmonic and coexact labels differ from those for
the complete cube. The field’s definition—fitness difference, transition log-odds, or another edge
statistic—must be written down before interpretation.
   Finally, “escape-proof” here means robust safety under a finite, specified relation. It is not a
statement of clinical certainty. In open biological systems, an adversary model is incomplete and a
finite landscape cannot enumerate all compensatory mutations. A practical goal is a transparent,
uncertainty-aware risk bound with explicit scope rather than a promise of indefinite real-world
prevention.

23 Conclusion
The proposed Hodge obstruction conjecture is not supported in its original form. If each drug’s edge
effect is a difference of a scalar fitness landscape, the resulting cochain is exact and every closed-loop
circulation vanishes. Hodge decomposition can expose inconsistency in noisy or differently defined
edge measurements, but no nonzero curl is available as a structural obstruction under this premise
and no drug-count lower bound follows from it.
   The existence of an escape-avoiding policy is instead characterized by the viability kernel of an
explicit controlled transition relation. In a finite model, the greatest fixed point gives an exact robust
safety guarantee, while a probabilistic dynamic program addresses finite-horizon risk under a specified
kernel. The seeded synthetic benchmark verifies both computations, demonstrates sensitivity to the
failure predicate, and separates exact mathematical identities from model-dependent outcomes. The
next meaningful step is to define the measured edge field, quantify uncertainty, and test whether any
Hodge-derived diagnostic improves held-out prediction or safety decisions beyond simpler landscape
and transition models.

A Proof details for the cubical consistency identity
For each locus pair i < j and each base genotype x whose bits i and j are zero, the oriented square
has vertices x, x + ei , x + ei + ej , and x + ej . Its circulation is

                      [f (x + ei ) − f (x)] + [f (x + ei + ej ) − f (x + ei )]
                                                                                                       (9)
                         + [f (x + ej ) − f (x + ei + ej )] + [f (x) − f (x + ej )] = 0.

Every term cancels once with its opposite. Algebraically this is B2 B0 = 0. The relation does not
depend on the numerical values of f , so it holds for any real-valued potential. The released code
uses least-squares projections to compute Hodge components and verifies that the exact field is
reconstructed within numerical tolerance.


B Algorithms and computational complexity
For n = |V |, m = |A|, and s = maxx,a |Succ(x, a)|, one naive predecessor iteration costs O(nms) after
successor sets are built. There are at most n strict elimination iterations, giving a simple upper
bound O(n2 ms); worklist-based algorithms may be faster on sparse systems. For horizon H, the
dynamic program costs O(Hmn2 ) with dense transition matrices and less with sparse representations.
Tabular Q-learning stores nm action-values and updates one entry per observed transition. Exact
belief-state enumeration can cost exponential time and memory, limiting use to small examples or
carefully designed abstractions.
   The safety and probability models must not be confused. The robust kernel uses only the support
set of possible successors; it does not care whether an allowed successor is very unlikely. The stochastic
dynamic program uses a probability distribution and minimizes expected hitting risk. In practical
systems, it may be valuable to compute both: robust safety identifies states safe under all modeled
possibilities, while the probabilistic calculation ranks policies when no robust solution exists or when
a probabilistic objective is operationally accepted.

D Additional methodological notes
The synthetic threshold is a formal predicate used to turn a continuous landscape into a reachability
target. It is fixed in the current run and exposed through sensitivity analysis. The deterministic
potential coefficients are disclosed in verify_model.py and are not fitted to real pathogen data.
Their role is to generate a nontrivial test instance where landscape geometry and action choice can be
inspected.
   No public empirical records or external datasets were downloaded into the release. The literature
