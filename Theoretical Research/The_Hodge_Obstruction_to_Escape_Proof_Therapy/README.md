## Research question

Can the presence of a nonzero Hodge-curl component in drug-specific collateral-effect fields determine whether an adaptive antimicrobial schedule can prevent a resistant population from entering a multi-drug-resistant failure set?

## Central correction

For a static drug-specific scalar fitness potential \(f_d\) on genotype vertices, define each oriented edge effect by endpoint differences:

$$
\omega_d(u,v)=f_d(v)-f_d(u).
$$

The resulting edge field is an exact discrete gradient. Its circulation around every closed path telescopes to zero; on a filled cubical genotype complex, the exact boundary-of-boundary identity also enforces:

$$
B_2 B_1 = 0.
$$

Therefore, **nonzero curl cannot be a fundamental Hodge obstruction for a correctly defined static scalar landscape**. Apparent nonzero curl can arise from measurement noise, inconsistent contexts, or genuine history-dependent edge effects that are not differences of one static potential. The proposed strong conjecture is not supported in its original form.

The defensible analysis instead combines discrete Hodge diagnostics with an explicit finite-state safety game. With safe set \(S\), action set \(A(x)\), and adversarially uncertain successor set \(\Gamma(x,a)\), the robust viability iteration is:

$$
K_{0}=S,\qquad K_{t+1}=\{x\in K_t:\exists a\in A(x),\;\Gamma(x,a)\subseteq K_t\}.
$$

The fixed point \(K_\infty\) is the robust winning region for the *specified finite model*, not a guarantee about real pathogens.

## What was tested

`verify_model.py` constructs a synthetic four-locus genotype hypercube and three toy drug-specific fitness potentials, checks the exact-gradient and boundary-of-boundary identities, decomposes noisy edge observations, computes a robust viability kernel, and tests finite-horizon escape-risk calculations. Run:

```bash
python verify_model.py
```

The deterministic model run reports a maximum exact square-circulation residual of approximately \(1.11\times 10^{-16}\), \(\|B_2B_1\|_F=0\), and a robust viability kernel of 4 of 16 genotypes under the benchmark’s disclosed assumptions. These are **synthetic-model results**. The simulated kernel and policies are not validated antimicrobial schedules, and the benchmark does not establish biological efficacy or safety.

Pinned/reference environment: Python 3.12, NumPy 2.3.5, Matplotlib 3.10.8, NetworkX 3.6.1, PyYAML 6.0.3, PyMuPDF 1.26.7. The verifier needs NumPy, Matplotlib, and NetworkX. Exact versions from the actual execution environment are recorded in `results/environment_report.json`.

## Research claims and limitations

- The exact-gradient / zero-circulation statement is an algebraic identity under the stated definition.
- The robust viability kernel is exact only for the finite transition model supplied to the solver.
- Learned landscape completion and robust reinforcement learning are proposed extensions, not demonstrations in the present benchmark.
- No real pathogen was cultured, sequenced, experimentally perturbed, or therapeutically treated for this paper.
- No empirical escape-proof therapy, general lower bound on the number of drugs, or clinical recommendation is claimed.
- Literature coverage is targeted, not exhaustive; the novelty claim is a formal synthesis and corrective no-go framing, not a verified first-ever discovery.
