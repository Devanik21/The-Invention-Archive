\begin{abstract}
The dissemination of post-PhD theoretical research demands mathematically rigorous verification and permanent, multi-channel deployment. We formalize an autonomous academic engine that enforces zero-cheating numerical validation, exact cryptographic provenance, and global schema indexing for weekly Saturday preprints. By modeling knowledge diffusion as an operator on a network manifold, we demonstrate that a structured deployment protocol—incorporating LaTeX preprints, executable Python numerical proofs, Zenodo DOI minting, Schema.org JSON-LD embeddings, and high-signal Medium essays—guarantees asymptotic synchronization across the academic digital footprint. This paper completely avoids LinkedIn, preserving the integrity of the theoretical research posture while strictly routing deployments to \texttt{The-Invention-Archive}.
\end{abstract}
