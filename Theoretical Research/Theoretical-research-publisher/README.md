## Abstract
The dissemination of post-PhD theoretical research demands mathematically rigorous verification and permanent, multi-channel deployment. We formalize an autonomous academic engine that enforces zero-cheating numerical validation, exact cryptographic provenance, and global schema indexing for weekly Saturday preprints. By modeling knowledge diffusion as an operator on a network manifold, we demonstrate that a structured deployment protocol guarantees asymptotic synchronization across the academic digital footprint. This architecture strictly routes deployments to `The-Invention-Archive`.
