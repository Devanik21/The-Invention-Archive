# Publication-quality report

## Automated observations
- PDF parses with PyMuPDF; 29 pages; A4 geometry.
- PDF font inspection (`pdffonts`) reports embedded fonts for the listed typefaces.
- Main text: pages 4–25, 22 pages, each with extracted text; title/contents precede it; appendix and references follow.
- Five LaTeX table environments/captions are present.
- Twelve numbered figure environments/captions are present and link to twelve PDF figure assets; all have corresponding PNG previews. The final tropical roundoff diagnostic uses a categorical count plot rather than a high-frequency connected residual trace.
- Verifier regenerated figure assets and CSV/JSON numerical outputs; verification exit code was zero.
- All pages rendered at 1.25x and assembled into three contact sheets; the contact sheets were reviewed. The contact-sheet review supports overall consistency and absence of obvious major defects, but it is not equivalent to a forensic close inspection of every glyph on every individual page.

## Residual limitation
