# Literature audit — 2026-10-31

## Scope and novelty posture
Searches covered o-minimality and tame geometry in string/flux landscapes; sharp o-minimality, format/degree, and Betti bounds; definable dynamical systems and invariants; autocatalytic/RAF network structure; and tropical equilibrations / biochemical reaction networks. The search was exploratory, not exhaustive. Record-level negative searches in MathSciNet and zbMATH were not completed; therefore the manuscript makes no claim that no prior work exists on o-minimality and biology.

## Verified core references
1. Grimm & Monnee (2024), *Finiteness theorems and counting conjectures for the flux landscape*, JHEP 08 (2024) 039, DOI `10.1007/JHEP08(2024)039`, arXiv:2311.09295. Supports use of tame geometry for specific string/flux-vacuum families and finite connected-component statements under hypotheses.
2. Grimm (2022), *Taming the landscape of effective theories*, JHEP 11 (2022) 003, DOI `10.1007/JHEP11(2022)003`, arXiv:2112.08383. Supports the proposed parameter-space/tame-geometry perspective in high-energy theory.
3. Binyamini, Novikov & Zack (2022), *Sharply o-minimal structures and sharp cellular decomposition*, arXiv:2209.10972 (version updated 2026-03-22). Supports format/degree-sensitive complexity and sharp cell-decomposition bounds; applying a particular sharp structure still requires proving definability and tracking format/degree.
4. Almagor, Chistikov, Ouaknine & Worrell (2022), *O-Minimal Invariants for Discrete-Time Dynamical Systems*, ACM Transactions on Computational Logic 23(2), Article 9, DOI `10.1145/3501299`. Important adjacent precedent; precludes a broad novelty claim about o-minimality and dynamical invariants.
5. Blokhuis, Lacoste & Nghe (2020), *Universal motifs and the diversity of autocatalytic systems*, PNAS 117(41):25230–25236, DOI `10.1073/pnas.2013527117`. Establishes five classes of minimal autocatalytic cores in the studied framework.
6. Nandan, Nghe & Unterberger (2026), *Autocatalytic cores in the diluted regime: classification and properties*, Journal of Mathematical Biology 92, Article 36, DOI `10.1007/s00285-026-02357-7`. Recent classification result; not a theorem for all possible chemistries or physical laws.
7. Steel, Hordijk & Smith (2013), *Minimal autocatalytic networks*, Journal of Theoretical Biology 332:96–107, DOI `10.1016/j.jtbi.2013.04.032`.
8. Hordijk & Steel (2019), *Autocatalytic networks in biology: structural theory and algorithms*, Journal of the Royal Society Interface, DOI `10.1098/rsif.2018.0829`. Supports RAF-network context and the caveat that network structure alone is not sufficient to establish life.
9. Desoeuvres, Szmolyan & Radulescu (2022), *Qualitative Dynamics of Chemical Reaction Networks: An Investigation Using Partial Tropical Equilibrations*, LNCS 13447, pp. 61–85, DOI `10.1007/978-3-031-15034-0_4`.
10. Desoeuvres et al. (2024), *Reduction of Chemical Reaction Networks with Approximate Conservation Laws*, SIAM Journal on Applied Dynamical Systems 23(1):256–296, DOI `10.1137/22M1543963`.
11. Cobley (2026), *A tropical geometry for bounded biochemical state spaces*, Patterns 7(10), Article 101613, DOI `10.1016/j.patter.2026.101613`.
12. van den Dries (1998), *Tame Topology and O-Minimal Structures*, Cambridge University Press.

## Contribution boundary
The paper proposes a formal synthesis: define a finite-dimensional family of law/parameter/topology tuples; encode repair-closure and a chosen autocatalytic witness as first-order definable predicates; apply o-minimal component finiteness only after definability is established; track sharp format/degree as a complexity ledger when a specific sharp structure supports it; and overlay tropical walls for a finite-support asymptotic approximation. The tropical layer and five-core classification are known ingredients. The proposed interface is a research programme, not an established theorem for arbitrary chemistry or every physical law-set.
