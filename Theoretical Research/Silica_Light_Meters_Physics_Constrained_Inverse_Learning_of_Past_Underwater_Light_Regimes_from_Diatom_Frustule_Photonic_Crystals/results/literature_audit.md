# Targeted literature audit

Search scope: public primary research, university thesis records, publisher pages and authoritative project descriptions searched on 2026-10-18. The audit is targeted rather than exhaustive; it is not a systematic review.

| Source | What it supports | What it does not support |
|---|---|---|
| Ferrara et al. 2014, PLOS ONE, DOI `10.1371/journal.pone.0103750` | Experimental optical effects in `Arachnoidiscus` valves, including wavelength-dependent intensity patterns | A fossil light proxy or general light-preference relation |
| Chen et al. 2015, Scientific Reports, DOI `10.1038/srep11977` | RCWA/FDTD light-trapping calculations and comparison with experiments | The proposed inverse ecological model |
| Romann et al. 2015, Scientific Reports, DOI `10.1038/srep17403` | Wavelength- and orientation-dependent capture in frustule structures | A calibrated pale-light reconstruction |
| Welchans 2020, University of Nebraska–Lincoln thesis | A single-species, single-Amazon-lake, indirect pore-size/light relationship for `Aulacoseira crenulata` | Multispecies generality, causal mechanism, or phylogenetically controlled transfer |
| Kloster et al. 2018, Marine Micropaleontology, DOI `10.1016/j.marmicro.2018.07.002` | `Fragilariopsis kerguelensis` morphotypes and temperature-linked glacial/interglacial core pattern | A light-specific proxy |
| Glemser et al. 2019, Polar Biology, DOI `10.1007/s00300-019-02525-0` | Morphotype frequencies in recent samples vary along environmental/geographic gradients | A universal morphometric proxy or proof of adaptive mechanism |
| Hofmann et al. 2020, Journal of Paleolimnology, DOI `10.1007/s10933-020-00139-9` | Diatom depth distributions depend on light, substrate and lake conditions; sediment assemblages need ecological context | Added value of optical simulation |
| Weiskerger et al. 2018, Water Resources Research, DOI `10.1029/2018WR023024` | Beer–Lambert-style PAR attenuation can be useful in shallow eutrophic waters, with constituent-specific variation | Universal adequacy of simple exponential attenuation |
| Kociolek, Ashworth & Alverson 2026, Journal of Phycology, DOI `10.1111/jpy.70125` | Diatom classification benefits from phylogenetic information; morphology-only groups may not mirror monophyly | A ready-made tree for the selected study taxa |
| Felsenstein 1985, American Naturalist, DOI `10.1086/284325`; Freckleton et al. 2002, DOI `10.1086/343873` | Standard reasons and methods for accounting for phylogenetic covariance | A guarantee that any one covariance model is correct |

Main gap: test whether physics-derived spectral features supply incremental, calibrated, out-of-sample information beyond raw morphology after ancestry and habitat controls. Claim novelty only at this proposed integration/validation level, subject to a fuller review.
