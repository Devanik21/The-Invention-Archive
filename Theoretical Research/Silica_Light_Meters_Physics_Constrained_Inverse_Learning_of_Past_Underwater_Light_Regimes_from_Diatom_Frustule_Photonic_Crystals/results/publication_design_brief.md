# Publication design brief

- **Audience:** interdisciplinary readers in diatom ecology, optical physics, paleolimnology and scientific machine learning.
- **Status/venue:** research preprint; no journal submission claimed. Layout is a journal-style one-column technical manuscript, not a journal template.
- **Page:** A4 with 0.83-inch margins; restrained ink/navy, teal and slate palette with gold accent; body 11 pt LaTeX Latin Modern and embedded Type 1 fonts where available.
- **Hierarchy:** compact preprint kicker, strong multi-line title, author/affiliation, abstract, then numbered sections with consistent spacing.
- **Headers/footers:** small running title/date; centered page number; thin teal rule.
- **Equations:** numbered LaTeX displays with defined symbols; only constructs needed for forward optics, attenuation, hierarchical structure and identifiability.
- **Tables:** booktabs style, minimal rulework, wrapped explanatory text; captions above, compact but readable body.
- **Figures:** vector PDF assets plus PNG previews; consistent font sizes, axes and captions. Data plots are explicitly synthetic. Causal/flow figures are programmatic diagrams, not evidence.
- **References:** numeric citations via BibLaTeX/Biber; DOI-bearing records are included where checked against publisher or repository records. A targeted literature audit records source coverage and remaining verification limits.
- **Whitespace:** no decorative cover, no forced page filler; figures kept close to discussion and long tables designed to fit at readable size.
- **Quality gate:** actual compilation, PDF parsing/page count, citation checks, regenerated asset checks, rendered-page inspection and explicit evidence boundary.
