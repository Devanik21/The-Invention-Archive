# Problem 3: Evolutionary reachability

Status: not started.

Question. Rather than predicting one evolutionary trajectory, which evolutionary outcomes are mathematically accessible in a rugged, epistatic landscape, which are not, and what constraints separate them?

Why it is open. Epistasis, evolutionary contingency, environmental feedback and stochasticity together prevent simple optimisation models from describing evolution.

Planned first step. Literature review of accessibility and reachability results in fitness landscapes, with a small exhaustive enumeration on a low-dimensional landscape as a first computation. No work has begun.
