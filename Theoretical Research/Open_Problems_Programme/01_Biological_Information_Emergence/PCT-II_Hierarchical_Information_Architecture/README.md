# PCT-II: Hierarchical Information Architecture

Status: not started. Pre-registration not yet written.

Question. At matched replication noise and matched catalytic density, does a two-level architecture (sequence -> catalytic function -> product sequence, with error correction at each level) carry more heritable information than a single-level architecture?

Why it matters. Carter and Wills' two-set cooperation and Iizuka's layered analogy both predict a hierarchical advantage. Neither gives a measurable quantity or a null.

Planned design, to be fixed before running.
- Compare one-level and two-level channels with identical total replication noise and catalyst density.
- Use the PCT-I machinery (exact Blahut-Arimoto capacity) and a held-out generator.
- Include a decoupled control and a shuffled-architecture control.
- Kill criterion: no advantage under matched conditions abandons the hierarchy hypothesis.

Dependency. Requires a PCT-I conclusion on the single-level channel, which is now in place.
