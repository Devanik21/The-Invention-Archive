# Burst-Robust Codes for Life and Qubits — Master v2.0

This is the expanded master research edition, stored alongside (not over) the original concise release.

## Contents
- `paper.pdf`: compiled 28–40 page A4 manuscript.
- `paper.tex`, `paper.bib`: complete LaTeX source and bibliography.
- `verify_model.py`, `deploy.py`: deterministic model reproduction and fail-closed release validation.
- `figures/`: 12 numbered vector scientific figures and PNG previews (plots, graphs and flows).
- `results/`: numerical sweeps, exact occupancy calibration, verification report, literature audit, claim-evidence matrix, PDF text index, all-page renderings, contact sheets, visual-review record, environment report, and SHA-256 manifest.
- `Master_v2.0_complete_bundle.zip`: full release archive.

The benchmark is a synthetic 24×24 geometric-connectivity surrogate. It is not a physical surface-code or holographic decoder, radiation-transport simulation, or biological experiment. The queueing and thinning statements are conditional on their stated assumptions. This is a research preprint, not a peer-reviewed journal publication.

Build and validation are performed by the repository's `.github/workflows/build-burst-robust-master-v2.yml` workflow.
