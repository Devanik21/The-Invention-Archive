# Publication design brief

- **Audience:** Quantum information, radiation effects, mathematical biology, reliability engineering, spatial stochastic processes, and queueing theory.
- **Format:** A4 theoretical/synthetic-computational preprint; 31-page target build, with front matter, section-level contents, approximately 25–26 substantive main-text pages, technical appendices, and references.
- **Typography:** 11 pt Latin Modern with T1 encoding and microtype; 0.80-inch margins; 22 pt running-header height; consistent numbered equations and theorem/proposition environments.
- **Navigation:** Running short title, date/status in header, centered page number, linked section-level table of contents, numbered sections/subsections.
- **Palette:** restrained ink/navy, teal, coral, and pale neutral tones. Color indicates analytical channel/diagram categories rather than decorative emphasis.
- **Tables:** Seven content-bearing LaTeX tables: epistemic classification, statistical/domain correspondence, recovery metrics, benchmark protocol, primary numerical results, validation programme, and failure modes/evidence requirements.
- **Figures:** Twelve generated vector figures plus PNG previews: eight plots, two dependency/causal graphs, and two process-flow diagrams.
- **Equations:** Key assumptions are stated adjacent to queue stability, Poisson thinning, occupancy calibration, code-specific recovery, and statistical interval expressions.
- **Captions and citations:** Captions identify each figure’s inferential role and explicitly label synthetic/conceptual outputs. Bibliography uses DOI-bearing references where available.
- **Whitespace:** No ornamental blank pages. Figure pages have captions and nearby interpretive text; tables are booktabs-style with wrapped columns.
- **Acceptance principle:** Page count and asset counts are floors, not substitutes for legibility, reproducibility, scientific validity, or independent review.
