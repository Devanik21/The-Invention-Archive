# Publication design brief

- **Reader/venue style:** interdisciplinary computational biology / applied mathematics preprint; no venue submission or acceptance claimed.
- **Page geometry:** US Letter, 0.82-inch margins, 11-point body, 1.06 leading; compact but readable headers and footers.
- **Typography:** Times-compatible text/math with T1 font encoding and microtype; hierarchy uses restrained deep blue-green accent. All fonts must be checked in final PDF.
- **Front matter:** designed title page with full title/subtitle, accurate author metadata, version/date/status, scope caveat, structured abstract and keywords. Separate table of contents with roman numerals.
- **Navigation:** running header with paper-short-title and manuscript status; centered page numbers; colored section heading hierarchy; appendices and references styled consistently.
- **Math:** numbered equations for SDE, action, persistence, bridge objective, and controller cost; definitions before use; derivation appendix.
- **Tables:** booktabs style, varied column widths, no vertical rules; captions clarify synthetic versus literature-derived content.
- **Figures:** existing generated vector PDF plots/diagrams, 12 numbered elements, restrained consistent palette, informative captions, and in-text interpretation. Graph/flow visuals clearly marked conceptual.
- **Whitespace/float rules:** figures at text widths scaled for legibility; no ornamental padding; float barriers before topic shifts; appendix includes derivations and reproducibility procedure.
- **Scientific honesty:** negative RL benchmark and pulse-topology counterexample are prominent. No empirical-data results, no wet-lab claims, and no fabricated summary metrics.
- **Reference standard:** emulate scholarly hierarchy and legibility from the prior aging preprint without copying its prose, figures, or layout.
