## Novelty conclusion
Established prior art includes fabric logging, diagenetic impacts on chemistry/chronology, multi-proxy proxy-system models, Bayesian proxy integration, and microbial/soil mixed-source biomarkers. The proposed contribution is an explicit recoverability-first formulation treating fabric as a noisy, calibratable side observation of alteration/channel state; connecting response-space identifiability with uncertainty; and testing both point error and interval coverage under held-out synthetic caves and channel shift. That is a formal synthesis/proposed method, not an asserted discovery of a new physical mechanism. Related unpublished or differently worded work may overlap; no absolute priority claim is made.

## Data feasibility conclusion
A defensible empirical decoder requires aligned proxies, chronology, per-sample fabric/alteration labels, and a credible modern monitoring/instrumental target. SISALv3's published description does not guarantee this joint table. SISAL_monv1 may provide modern monitoring targets, but its underlying repository was not inspectable in this execution and proxy/fabric/chronology alignment remains unverified. Since row-level alignment has not been audited, empirical evaluation is **BLOCKED**, and all results in this release remain synthetic.
