# Publication design brief — version 1

- **Audience:** paleoclimate / speleothem science, statistical inverse problems, and scientific ML readers. No particular journal submission is claimed.
- **Page geometry:** A4; 25 mm margins; 11 pt body type.
- **Typography:** Latin Modern text/math and embedded DejaVu Sans axes/fonts in figures; restrained hierarchy (large title, 11 pt body, small captions/tables).
- **Header/footer:** simple running title in ink-navy and right-aligned page number; no decorative cover.
- **Color system:** ink navy `#17324D`, teal `#168C8C`, coral `#C95C54`, amber `#D6922E`, pale blue-gray and muted neutral lines. Color is not the only distinction in plotted categories.
- **Equations:** numbered amsmath displays, prose defines symbols at first use, references to equations in text.
- **Tables:** booktabs rules; no vertical grid; small but readable text with line-wrap columns and concise evidence captions.
- **Figures:** eight PDF/SVG vector source assets from the released generator; four data-bearing synthetic plots, two conceptual graph/geometry figures and two proposed process flows, consistently colored and numbered.
- **Captions:** identify synthetic/model-generated material and conceptual diagrams explicitly; text introduces and interprets each visual.
- **References:** natbib numeric citations close to supported claims, BibTeX source preserved.
- **Whitespace/layout policy:** normal LaTeX float placement and moderate paragraph separation. Do not shrink body type to meet visual counts. Any remaining underfull box warning is reviewed for visible effect rather than treated as a design pass.
