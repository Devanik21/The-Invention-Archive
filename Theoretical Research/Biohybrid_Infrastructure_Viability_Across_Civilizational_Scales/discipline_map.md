# Eighteen-Discipline Map

| Discipline | Model object / variable | Engineering contribution | Validation route |
|---|---|---|---|
| Synthetic biology | Host function, phenotype, containment | Engineered functions and production hosts | Strain-specific data, containment and stability |
| Systems biology | Coupled biochemical state | Dynamic response and internal regulation | Time series, mass balance and perturbation |
| Photosynthesis | Photon flux, absorption and yield | Light-driven carbon fixation / conversion | Spectral and product-energy balances |
| Bioelectrochemistry | Current, potential, interface resistance | Electron transfer and electrochemical coupling | Polarization curves and charge balance |
| Metabolic engineering | Stoichiometry and flux vector | Product pathway design | Flux, yield and metabolite measurements |
| Microbiology | Biomass and reaction kinetics | Waste conversion, leaching and bioremediation | Controlled reactor experiments |
| Biomaterials | Constitutive law and microstructure | Biological and hybrid material design | Tensile, compression, fracture and aging |
| Biomineralization | Supersaturation and deposition | Mineral binding and sealing | Mineralogy, deposition and mechanical tests |
| Mycology | Hyphal geometry and composite structure | Fungal composite manufacture | Imaging and mechanical/thermal tests |
| Ecology | Trophic and resource interactions | Regenerative ecosystem architecture | Stability, diversity and perturbation |
| Bioprocess engineering | Reactor geometry and mass transfer | Reactor design and scale-up | Transfer coefficients and productivity |
| Chemical engineering | Reaction, separation and transport | Abiotic baseline and downstream purification | Unit-operation data and process simulation |
| Materials science | Strength, fatigue and thermal response | Structural and surface qualification | Standardized material tests |
| Aerospace engineering | Orbit, gravity, pressure and radiation | Space environment and structural constraints | Mission analysis and environmental tests |
| Robotics | Agents, tasks, motion and repair state | Construction, inspection and maintenance | Hardware-in-loop and fault injection |
| Control theory | Dynamics, constraints and policy | Stability and fault management | Stability analysis and closed-loop tests |
| Thermodynamics | Energy, exergy and entropy | Feasibility and waste-heat bounds | First- and second-law accounting |
| Artificial intelligence | Surrogates, uncertainty and policy | Search, inference and adaptive operation | Held-out benchmarks and calibration |

The discipline count describes programme coverage. It does not imply that every module should combine all eighteen. A discipline is used only when it supplies a real variable, constraint, method or validation requirement.
