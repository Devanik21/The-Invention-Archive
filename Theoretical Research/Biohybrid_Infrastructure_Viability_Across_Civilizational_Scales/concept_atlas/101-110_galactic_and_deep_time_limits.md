# Phase VI — Galactic and Deep-Time Limits

## 101. Galactic distributed bio-refineries

**Class / status:** B/S

Scenario of protected biological manufacturing nodes at suitable habitats; no biology is assumed in interstellar vacuum.

## 102. Stellar-cluster autonomous maintenance swarms

**Class / status:** M/S

Study local robotic coordination across multiple stellar systems with communication constrained by light travel time.

## 103. Galactic infrastructure digital twins

**Class / status:** M/S

Model resource, energy, heat and maintenance flows across stellar systems; uncertainty and latency dominate.

## 104. Multi-system biosphere resilience

**Class / status:** B/M/S

Study independent settlements and ecological compartment redundancy as a resilience problem, not guaranteed survival.

## 105. Planetary biomanufacturing establishment kits

**Class / status:** B/H/S

Develop standardized contained systems for commissioning local biological production once habitat support infrastructure exists.

## 106. Distributed DNA knowledge archives

**Class / status:** B/H/S

Explore redundant information storage in DNA alongside conventional digital records and integrity checks.

## 107. Post-biological cellular-automata infrastructure

**Class / status:** M/S

Translate local growth and communication rules into nonliving autonomous computing/manufacturing networks.

## 108. Fractal vascular energy networks

**Class / status:** M/S

Study hierarchical graph structures for routing power and heat among large nodes under finite capacity.

## 109. Deep-time biological preservation networks

**Class / status:** B/S

Assess long-term preservation, inspection, redundancy and reactivation of reference collections; no eternal preservation assumption.

## 110. Cosmological infrastructure viability limits

**Class / status:** M/S

Bound hypothetical civilization-scale systems using energy availability, entropy generation, causal horizons and finite information transfer. Type VI is a nonstandard thought-experiment label.

These entries are concepts for feasibility screening only. Each requires a source audit, explicit system boundary, abiotic comparison and falsification criterion before entering the quantitative model.
