# Phase V — Interstellar Infrastructure

## 86. Generation-ship ecological engines

**Class / status:** B/H/P

Model long-duration food, atmosphere, water and waste loops in a pressurized rotating habitat with multigenerational service requirements.

## 87. Interstellar microbial manufacturing libraries

**Class / status:** B/P

Store contained, characterized production strains for activation only after destination and containment checks.

## 88. Dormant biomanufacturing payloads

**Class / status:** B/P

Test long-term preservation and post-activation performance of sealed biological production cartridges.

## 89. Cell-free manufacturing cartridges

**Class / status:** H/P

Use preserved enzymes and cell-free systems for selected synthesis where avoiding viable-cell containment is valuable.

## 90. Distributed protein manufacturing

**Class / status:** B/P

Produce defined protein products from local carbon/nitrogen feedstocks; include purification, quality and energy.

## 91. Bio-inspired logistics across settlements

**Class / status:** M/P

Use decentralized routing for local settlement logistics under communication delay; avoid assuming instantaneous interstellar control.

## 92. DNA-based scientific archives

**Class / status:** H/P

Evaluate DNA storage as a dense archive, counting synthesis, sequencing, error correction, redundancy and radiation shielding.

## 93. Mycelial-topology communication algorithms

**Class / status:** M/P

Use network adaptation concepts from fungal systems for resilient routing among intermittent nodes.

## 94. Interstellar autonomous assembly swarms

**Class / status:** M/S

Study robotic modular construction with local sensing and delayed coordination; treat propulsion and orbital mechanics separately.

## 95. Photosynthetic energy–biomass coupling

**Class / status:** B/H/P

Couple nonliving solar power with contained photobiological production; compare against direct electricity and chemical synthesis.

## 96. Multi-century seed and microbial archives

**Class / status:** B/P

Design preservation with verified storage conditions, periodic inspection and validated reactivation; no indefinite dormancy assumption.

## 97. Extremophile-enzyme process catalysts

**Class / status:** B/H/P

Screen enzymes from terrestrial extremophiles for specific industrial windows; source-organism tolerance does not transfer automatically.

## 98. Interstellar biogeochemical recycling

**Class / status:** B/H/P

Maintain carbon, nitrogen, phosphorus and water cycles in contained settlement modules with redundancy.

## 99. Distributed asteroid bio-refineries

**Class / status:** B/H/S

Model local biomining nodes paired with abiotic separation and robotic logistics; site and ore composition determine feasibility.

## 100. Interstellar vascular energy networks

**Class / status:** M/S

Apply hierarchical network design to power, coolant and material distribution among local installations; no faster-than-light coordination.

These entries are concepts for feasibility screening only. Each requires a source audit, explicit system boundary, abiotic comparison and falsification criterion before entering the quantitative model.
