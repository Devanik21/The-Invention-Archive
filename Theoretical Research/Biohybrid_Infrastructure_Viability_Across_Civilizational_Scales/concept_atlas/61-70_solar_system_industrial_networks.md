# Phase IIIB — Solar-System Industrial Networks

## 61. Vascular-inspired thermal distribution

**Class / status:** M/P

Optimize branching coolant networks for pressure loss, mass, redundancy, inspection and isolation.

## 62. Mycelium-inspired resource routing

**Class / status:** M/P

Develop decentralized algorithms for routing material and energy flows; compare with conventional network optimization.

## 63. Self-cleaning orbital collector surfaces

**Class / status:** M/P

Use low-adhesion synthetic textures and active cleaning to reduce contaminant accumulation on nonliving collectors.

## 64. Compound-eye-inspired optical collectors

**Class / status:** M/P

Explore segmented wide-angle optical systems inspired by compound eyes; compare optical efficiency and fabrication complexity.

## 65. Sunflower-inspired solar tracking

**Class / status:** M/P

Use distributed sensing and control for collector orientation; account for pointing loss, actuator energy and failure.

## 66. Biomimetic redox energy storage

**Class / status:** H/P

Study quinone-inspired or enzyme-inspired redox chemistries for storage; compare lifetime, safety and energy density.

## 67. Enzyme-assisted refining steps

**Class / status:** B/H/P

Use selective catalysts for particular purification reactions downstream of primary abiotic extraction.

## 68. Biologically inspired ion-selective interfaces

**Class / status:** M/H/P

Develop synthetic ion-binding surfaces for targeted separation; measure selectivity, capacity and regeneration energy.

## 69. Bio-composite electrical insulation

**Class / status:** H/P

Test cellulose or biopolymer insulation only for suitable controlled environments; measure dielectric breakdown and outgassing.

## 70. Regolith biomineralization repair networks

**Class / status:** H/P

Use contained mineral deposition to repair noncritical regolith components and stabilize surfaces; validate strength and water budget.

These entries are concepts for feasibility screening only. Each requires a source audit, explicit system boundary, abiotic comparison and falsification criterion before entering the quantitative model.
