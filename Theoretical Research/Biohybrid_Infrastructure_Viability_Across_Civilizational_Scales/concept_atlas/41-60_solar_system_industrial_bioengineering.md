# Phase IIIA — Solar-System Industrial Bioengineering

## 41. Gravity-independent gas-exchange networks

**Class / status:** M/P

Design reactor manifolds and membrane contactors for gas transfer without relying on buoyancy-driven mixing.

## 42. Leaf-vein photobioreactor manifolds

**Class / status:** M/H/P

Use branching channels to distribute nutrients and coolant in large photobioreactors; optimize pumping, material and illumination.

## 43. Bacterial-cellulose crop supports

**Class / status:** B/H/P

Produce porous root-support and water-distribution matrices; measure wet strength, decomposition and reuse.

## 44. Bio-manufactured industrial sealants

**Class / status:** B/H/P

Produce polymer sealants for noncritical joints; test vacuum compatibility, outgassing, thermal cycling and adhesion.

## 45. Gecko-inspired regolith anchoring

**Class / status:** M/P

Use dry adhesive or interlocking microstructures for robotic attachment to granular terrain; test dust and temperature effects.

## 46. Lunar-dust-resistant surface textures

**Class / status:** M/P

Engineer low-adhesion, abrasion-resistant surfaces for optics and collectors; measure dust adhesion and cleaning energy.

## 47. Hydrogen-rich polymer shielding feedstocks

**Class / status:** H/P

Assess locally produced hydrogen-rich polymers as one radiation-shielding option against water and regolith at equal areal mass.

## 48. Root-inspired regolith retention

**Class / status:** M/P

Use interconnected nonliving fibres to stabilize granular excavation walls and berms; test shear strength and dust behaviour.

## 49. Biological atmospheric-quality sensors

**Class / status:** H/P

Use contained biosensors for selected air contaminants; quantify cross-sensitivity, calibration drift and containment failure.

## 50. Algal emergency food modules

**Class / status:** B/L/P

Develop compact reserve photobioreactors; evaluate nutrition, contamination, power, storage and time-to-product.

## 51. Selective bioadsorption of asteroid metals

**Class / status:** B/H/P

Use biological binding surfaces to concentrate selected ions from leachates; test selectivity, regeneration and final recovery.

## 52. Fungal mineral extraction

**Class / status:** B/L/P

Measure fungal acid and chelator pathways against bacterial and abiotic mineral leaching for specified substrates.

## 53. Asteroid-ore bio-beneficiation

**Class / status:** B/H/P

Use selective surface interactions to pre-concentrate mineral phases before conventional refining; quantify grade and recovery.

## 54. Microbial biopolymer additive manufacturing

**Class / status:** B/P

Produce defined polymer feedstocks from local carbon; assess printability, sterilization, strength and product mass balance.

## 55. Bacterial-cellulose filter factories

**Class / status:** B/H/P

Manufacture replaceable filtration media for controlled air/water processing with quality control and waste accounting.

## 56. Bio-derived assembly adhesives

**Class / status:** B/H/P

Develop polymer adhesives for interior or non-pressure-bearing assemblies; characterize ageing and vacuum outgassing.

## 57. Enzymatic polymer-recycling plants

**Class / status:** B/H/P

Use substrate-specific enzymes to depolymerize selected waste polymers; quantify reaction conditions, monomer purity and enzyme lifetime.

## 58. Microbial lubricant precursors

**Class / status:** B/P

Investigate biosynthesis of lubricant molecules; compare rheology, oxidation, outgassing and temperature range with conventional products.

## 59. Algae-to-bioplastic production

**Class / status:** B/H/P

Convert algal carbon feedstock into polymer precursors; include harvesting, drying, extraction and end-of-life treatment.

## 60. Waste-gas-to-feedstock networks

**Class / status:** B/H/P

Convert suitable gas streams into chemical intermediates using biological or catalytic units; balance carbon, redox and energy.

These entries are concepts for feasibility screening only. Each requires a source audit, explicit system boundary, abiotic comparison and falsification criterion before entering the quantitative model.
