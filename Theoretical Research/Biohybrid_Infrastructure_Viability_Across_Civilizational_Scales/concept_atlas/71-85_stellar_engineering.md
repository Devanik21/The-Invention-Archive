# Phase IV — Stellar Engineering

## 71. Leaf-venation collector interconnects

**Class / status:** M/P

Apply branching conductor topology to nonliving solar collectors; optimize conductor mass, resistance and fault isolation.

## 72. Chloroplast-inspired photonic textures

**Class / status:** M/P

Use nonliving nanoscale optical structures to improve light trapping in photovoltaic materials; benchmark against planar devices.

## 73. Self-repairing collector skins

**Class / status:** M/P

Use replaceable patches, microcapsule polymers or robotic repair inspired by skin repair; test vacuum and radiation durability.

## 74. Ant-colony-inspired swarm maintenance

**Class / status:** M/P

Distribute inspection and replacement tasks across autonomous collector units; test communication loss and congestion.

## 75. Insect-cuticle-inspired radiator surfaces

**Class / status:** M/P

Develop hierarchical spectral surfaces for controlled infrared emission; quantify emissivity and ageing.

## 76. Vascular electrical distribution

**Class / status:** M/P

Optimize branching conductor networks among collector modules; include resistive loss, thermal loading and redundancy.

## 77. Protected orbital biomanufacturing stations

**Class / status:** B/H/P

Use shielded pressurized factories to produce selected nutrients, polymers or chemical intermediates for local industry.

## 78. Modular O'Neill biosphere architecture

**Class / status:** B/H/P

Separate ecological functions into fault-isolated modules with backup stores and tested recovery policies.

## 79. Fungal-inspired internal habitat panels

**Class / status:** H/M/P

Use mycelium composites or nonliving geometric analogues for acoustic, interior and insulation functions only after qualification.

## 80. Closed-loop O'Neill agriculture and atmosphere

**Class / status:** B/H/P

Integrate crops, algal reactors, water treatment and nutrient recycling; quantify area, power, buffer mass and crop resilience.

## 81. Distributed stellar collector meshes

**Class / status:** M/S

Study orientation, orbital distribution and network coordination of independent nonliving collector units.

## 82. Asteroid-to-collector bio-refineries

**Class / status:** B/H/P

Evaluate contained biomining as an upstream resource step; all final metal refining and collector fabrication require conventional processes.

## 83. Collector health-monitoring skins

**Class / status:** H/M/P

Use embedded nonliving sensors or contained indicators to detect cracking, thermal stress and electrical degradation.

## 84. Distributed megastructure logistics

**Class / status:** M/P

Use decentralized scheduling for maintenance, spare parts and robot allocation across remote modules.

## 85. Multi-biosphere habitat networks

**Class / status:** B/H/P

Study independent ecological compartments and cross-module reserve strategies to limit cascading habitat failure.

These entries are concepts for feasibility screening only. Each requires a source audit, explicit system boundary, abiotic comparison and falsification criterion before entering the quantitative model.
