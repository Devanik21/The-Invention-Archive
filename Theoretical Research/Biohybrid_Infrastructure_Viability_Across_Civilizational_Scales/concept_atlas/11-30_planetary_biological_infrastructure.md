# Phase I — Planetary Biological Infrastructure

## 11. Mycorrhizal urban water networks

**Class / status:** B/P

Study contained plant–fungal associations for soil-water retention and nutrient exchange; quantify irrigation savings and ecosystem side effects.

## 12. Algal wastewater nutrient refineries

**Class / status:** B/E

Recover nitrogen and phosphorus from wastewater in algal reactors; count harvesting, dewatering and nutrient-quality requirements.

## 13. Bacterial-cellulose filtration membranes

**Class / status:** B/H/E

Grow cellulose membranes and test permeability, fouling, regeneration and mechanical lifetime against conventional filters.

## 14. Aquaporin-inspired desalination

**Class / status:** M/H/P

Use selective protein-channel principles in synthetic membranes; quantify salt rejection, flux, stability and membrane replacement.

## 15. Microbial electrosynthesis refineries

**Class / status:** H/E

Couple electroactive microbes to supplied electricity and carbon feedstocks for chemical production; track electron and carbon efficiency.

## 16. Microbial fuel-cell sensor nodes

**Class / status:** H/E

Use low-power bioelectrochemical outputs for local sensing in wastewater or remote environments; do not claim grid-scale power.

## 17. Biological nitrogen-recycling infrastructure

**Class / status:** B/E

Integrate fixation, nitrification, denitrification and recovery with complete nitrogen balance and emission monitoring.

## 18. Microbial carbon-to-mineral systems

**Class / status:** B/E

Evaluate microbially assisted carbonate mineralization with stable-storage verification and full reagent/energy accounting.

## 19. Phytomining of critical metals

**Class / status:** B/E

Test metal-accumulating plants on suitable low-grade feedstocks; include concentration factors, harvest cost and recovery losses.

## 20. Root-inspired landscape stabilization

**Class / status:** M/E

Use engineered fibre networks or root-reinforced substrates for erosion control; distinguish living roots from nonliving mimics.

## 21. Insect-based organic-waste bioconversion

**Class / status:** B/E

Convert selected organic residues into usable biomass and soil amendments under pathogen, feedstock and lifecycle controls.

## 22. Precision-fermented proteins

**Class / status:** B/E

Produce defined food proteins in controlled fermentation; compare edible product, purification, carbon/nitrogen input and energy.

## 23. Contained biological contamination sentinels

**Class / status:** H/P

Use sealed biosensor modules to detect selected contaminants through calibrated electrical or optical signals.

## 24. Microbiome-optimized vertical farming

**Class / status:** B/E

Test plant-associated microbial consortia for crop yield stability, not isolated growth effects or uncontrolled microbiome claims.

## 25. Mangrove-inspired water separation

**Class / status:** M/P

Design hierarchical synthetic membranes inspired by selective salt and water transport; validate pressure, fouling and durability.

## 26. Termite-mound-inspired thermal buildings

**Class / status:** M/E

Use passive ventilation, thermal mass and controlled airflow channels to lower building cooling demand under stated climates.

## 27. Self-healing bioconcrete

**Class / status:** H/E

Test contained mineral-producing systems for small-crack repair; measure depth, strength recovery, durability and safety.

## 28. Lotus-inspired self-cleaning surfaces

**Class / status:** M/E

Use low-adhesion microtextures to limit dirt and water accumulation on panels, windows and industrial surfaces.

## 29. Shark-skin-inspired flow coatings

**Class / status:** M/E

Test riblet-like textures for drag reduction or fouling control only in the Reynolds-number and flow regimes where they apply.

## 30. Fungal mycoremediation units

**Class / status:** B/E

Use characterized fungi to transform selected organics; measure metabolites and toxicity so disappearance is not mistaken for detoxification.

These entries are concepts for feasibility screening only. Each requires a source audit, explicit system boundary, abiotic comparison and falsification criterion before entering the quantitative model.
