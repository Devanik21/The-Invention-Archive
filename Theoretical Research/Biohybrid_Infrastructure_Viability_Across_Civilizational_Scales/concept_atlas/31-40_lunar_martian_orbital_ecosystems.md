# Phase II — Lunar, Martian and Orbital Ecosystems

## 31. Perchlorate-treatment bioreactors

**Class / status:** B/P

Investigate contained microbial conversion of perchlorates in characterized simulant feedstocks; verify residual species and byproducts.

## 32. Extraterrestrial oxygen-producing photobioreactors

**Class / status:** B/L/P

Operate algae or cyanobacteria only in controlled pressurized habitats; measure net oxygen, biomass, gas transfer and lighting power.

## 33. Biological methane-feedstock plants

**Class / status:** B/H/P

Evaluate microbial methane production from declared carbon and hydrogen sources; include feedstock preparation and gas cleanup.

## 34. Regolith phosphorus recovery

**Class / status:** B/H/P

Test selective mobilization of phosphorus from characterized regolith simulants and verify plant-available product quality.

## 35. Lunar mineral-bioleaching modules

**Class / status:** B/L/P

Extend biomining methods to specific lunar simulants; compare element-by-element recovery with abiotic leaching.

## 36. Organic-waste mineralization factories

**Class / status:** H/P

Combine digestion, oxidation, filtration and nutrient recovery into a contained waste-to-feedstock line.

## 37. Dormant microbial emergency libraries

**Class / status:** B/P

Store characterized contained strains for activation after system checks; validate viability, genetic stability and contamination control.

## 38. Engineered root-zone ecosystems

**Class / status:** B/P

Use defined plant–microbe systems to improve nutrient uptake in controlled agriculture with measurable input/output balances.

## 39. Robotic pollination infrastructure

**Class / status:** M/P

Use small robots or mechanisms for pollination inside enclosed agriculture; compare energy, reliability and biological alternatives.

## 40. Living selective membrane systems

**Class / status:** H/P

Integrate transport proteins with synthetic supports; quantify selectivity, flux, lifetime, sterilization and fouling.

These entries are concepts for feasibility screening only. Each requires a source audit, explicit system boundary, abiotic comparison and falsification criterion before entering the quantitative model.
