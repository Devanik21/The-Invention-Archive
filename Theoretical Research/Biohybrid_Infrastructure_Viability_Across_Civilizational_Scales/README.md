# Biohybrid Infrastructure Viability Across Civilizational Scales

**Research programme · Proposal architecture / literature grounding · 2026-11-21**

A cross-scale biological-engineering programme asking where living, biohybrid, biomimetic and conventional abiotic technologies are useful in infrastructure—from regenerative planetary systems and space habitats to asteroid industry, orbital megastructures and physically bounded stellar-scale scenarios.

> **Status:** Programme-stage workspace, not a completed paper or validated technology portfolio. The 110 entries are a structured design atlas, not 110 established technologies. No experimental results, industrial-readiness claims or Type IV–VI civilizational categories are asserted.

## Selected research question

Under which measurable environmental, resource, reliability and lifecycle conditions does a biohybrid architecture become Pareto-nondominated relative to biological-only and abiotic-only alternatives?

## Main hypothesis

A nonempty, evidence-parameterized region may exist in which mixed architectures outperform pure alternatives by assigning biological processes only to functions where their full-system advantage survives accounting for reactor mass, feedstock, light, water, gas transfer, separation, purification, containment, maintenance, waste and heat rejection. This is falsifiable and not yet demonstrated.

## Scientific boundaries

- B (living biological), H (biohybrid), M (biomimetic) and A (conventional abiotic) are distinct engineering classes.
- Every comparison uses the same functional unit, operating envelope, system boundary and uncertainty treatment.
- Living systems are assumed to operate only in suitable, contained environments. No microbes are proposed for uncontained operation in vacuum.
- Dyson collectors, orbital radiators, exposed space structures and robots are nonliving engineered systems. Biology may support protected manufacturing or inspire their geometry and control.
- O'Neill cylinders and Dyson swarms are scenarios, not current capabilities.
- The original Kardashev scale has three broad types. Extensions beyond Type III are nonstandard extrapolations used only for thought experiments.
- Numerical model verification is not experimental or biological validation.

## Programme status

The candidate ranking is preliminary. The first research gate is a primary-literature audit and an evidence-calibrated comparison of regenerative life support, biohybrid photochemistry, biomining and biomaterials against abiotic baselines.
