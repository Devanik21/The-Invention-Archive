## Evidence categories

- Direct experimental evidence: measurement under a described test condition.
- Flight/spaceflight evidence: measurement in a spaceflight environment, with sample and replication limits.
- Review/perspective: useful for locating evidence and gaps, not an independent performance result.
- Engineering scenario: a design proposal with assumptions, not a demonstrated system.
- Mathematical consequence: follows from the declared model, not automatically from nature.

## Immediate literature gaps

1. Life-support comparison must include reactor, purification, lighting and maintenance mass on a common functional basis.
2. Space bioleaching needs element- and organism-specific data extraction and uncertainty treatment.
3. Mycelium and MICP applications need specific test conditions and materials data; terrestrial performance cannot be transferred directly to vacuum/low gravity.
4. Biohybrid energy must separate electrical and chemical output and compare products on an exergy-consistent basis.
5. The 110-concept atlas requires later triage; it is not evidence that each concept is novel.
