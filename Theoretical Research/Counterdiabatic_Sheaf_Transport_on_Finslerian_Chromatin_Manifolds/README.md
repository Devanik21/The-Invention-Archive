# Counterdiabatic Sheaf Transport on Finslerian Chromatin Manifolds

**Subtitle:** An Identity-Constrained, Energy-Aware Framework for Partial Cellular Rejuvenation  
**Authors:** Devanik Debnath and Xylia (AI-assisted research system)  
**Release date:** 2026-10-14

## Overview

This preprint proposes a falsifiable theoretical and computational framework combining directional state-space costs, regulatory-consistency constraints represented with cellular sheaves, port-Hamiltonian-inspired dynamics, and constrained control for studying partial cellular rejuvenation.

The framework is a research proposal, not an established biological mechanism or a validated rejuvenation intervention. Its mathematical and computational components are distinguished from empirical hypotheses throughout the manuscript.

## Scientific status and limitations

- The numerical demonstration uses a dimensionless synthetic toy model; it is not biological validation.
- No biological dataset was analyzed and no wet-lab experiment was conducted for this release.
- Sheaf consistency is not proof of lineage preservation, genomic integrity, or cancer safety.
- The framework requires independent empirical validation, including cell-identity, function, and safety endpoints.
- This manuscript has not been peer reviewed and has no DOI assigned as of this release.
