# Epsilon-Nets of Antigenic Space

**Conditional Bounds for Broad-Spectrum Antibody Cocktails**  
*Pfaffian and o-minimal range complexity, multi-covers, and the price of robust protection*

**Devanik Debnath** · Independent theoretical preprint · 1 November 2026 · Version 1.0.0  
[Research archive](https://github.com/Devanik21/The-Invention-Archive) · [ORCID](https://orcid.org/0009-0001-8310-6921)

> **Scope:** This is a conditional mathematical framework for passive antibody-cocktail selection. It is not a universal vaccine, a clinical recommendation, an empirically derived cocktail size, or evidence of escape-proof biological protection. The manuscript has not undergone peer review.

## Central question

Can the size of a sufficient antibody subset be bounded using the combinatorial complexity of target-indexed neutralization ranges and a lower bound on candidate-repertoire mass, rather than directly by the number of listed antigenic variants?

The phrase “diversity-free” is restricted to the absence of an explicit target-panel cardinality term while the VC dimension and lower-mass margin remain fixed. Biological diversity can reduce the minimum neutralizer mass, increase range complexity, or introduce zero-coverage targets.

## Mathematical core

Let `A` be a candidate antibody repertoire with probability measure `mu`; a target `x` defines a thresholded neutralization range:

$$
R_x(c,\theta)=\{a\in\mathcal A:\nu(a,x;c)\geq\theta\}.
$$

A Hill response model is used for the synthetic construction:

$$
\nu(a,x;c)=\frac{c^{h(a,x)}}{c^{h(a,x)}+\mathrm{IC}_{50}(a,x)^{h(a,x)}}.
$$

If every target range has mass at least `epsilon` under a fixed measure and the range family has VC dimension `d`, classical epsilon-net theory gives a sufficient selection bound:

$$
|S|=O\!\left(\frac{d}{\epsilon}\log\frac{1}{\epsilon}\right).
$$

The expression has no explicit target-count term while `d` and `epsilon` stay fixed. It is not measure-free, and it does not imply a biological bound unless the range model and the minimum-mass condition are established.

For finite-uniform candidate pools, the published epsilon-`k`-net theorem provides a family of `k`-subsets under finite-hypergraph, dual-shatter-growth, and sufficiently-large-pool assumptions. The union gives a conservative global multi-cover bound:

$$
|U|=O\!\left(\frac{k d(1+\log k)}{\epsilon}\log\frac{1}{\epsilon}\right).
$$

For weighted candidate measures, a separate sufficient result is proved under the small-atom condition:

$$
\eta\leq\frac{\epsilon}{2ks},\qquad |S|\leq ks,
$$
where `eta` is the largest atomic mass and `s` is a uniform residual epsilon/2-net bound. This is not a new general multi-cover theorem and is not claimed optimal.

A two-dimensional antigenic map does **not** imply VC dimension two or three. A disk-family value of three is used only in the synthetic toy model.

## What was verified

`verify_model.py` uses seed `20261101`, 4,000 synthetic antibody candidates, 72 synthetic targets, epsilon `0.20`, and multi-cover demand `k=2`. In the current deterministic finite instance:

- All 72 target ranges meet the stipulated epsilon-heavy condition.
- The greedy finite single-cover selected 3 candidates.
- The greedy finite double-cover selected 6 distinct candidates.
- The minimum achieved double-cover multiplicity is 2.
- All-range hit fractions from 250 without-replacement trials were 0.560 at sample size 8, 0.872 at 12, 0.968 at 16, 0.996 at 24, and 1.000 at sizes 32 through 192.

These results are synthetic and are not estimates of antibody requirements for HIV, snakebite, influenza, or any other biological setting. No CATNAP records were downloaded or analyzed and no antivenom assay data were used.

## Reproduce

Tested locally with Python 3.13.5, NumPy 2.3.5, Matplotlib 3.10.8, NetworkX 3.6.1, PyMuPDF 1.26.7, PyYAML 6.0.3, and pdfTeX 1.40.26.

```bash
python verify_model.py
python -m py_compile verify_model.py deploy.py
python deploy.py --self-test
python deploy.py --build
python deploy.py --validate-only
```

The generator is deterministic, needs no network or API credentials, and writes synthetic CSV/JSON evidence plus vector plots. The bibliography is manually typeset in `paper.tex` because a BibTeX executable was not installed in the authoring environment; citation keys are cross-checked against `paper.bib`.

## Data and limitations

- CATNAP was identified as a future empirical resource; the download endpoint was not accessible in the build environment. No rows were analyzed.
- No antivenom assay dataset was analyzed.
- MathSciNet's landing page loaded but the JavaScript search did not return a verifiable result set; zbMATH Open access was blocked. The literature review is targeted, not exhaustive.
- Finite VC dimension does not by itself provide a useful numeric `d`; quantitative Pfaffian bounds require an audited formula format.
- Distinct antibody counting does not ensure distinct epitopes or independent escape pathways.
- Active vaccination requires a host- and schedule-dependent immunogen-to-repertoire map, not direct antibody selection.
- Greedy finite cover sizes are feasible solutions, not proven minima.
