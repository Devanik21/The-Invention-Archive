# Publication design brief

**Reader / venue style:** mathematical biology, computational immunology, statistical learning theory, and theoretical foundations of biomedical design. Independent preprint; not submitted to or endorsed by a journal.

**Page system:** A4 portrait, 11 pt Latin Modern Roman with matching mathematics, microtype, approximately 0.82 in margins, consistent paragraph rhythm, numbered equations, restrained ink/teal/slate color system. No decorative cover image.

**Front matter:** balanced title page with title/subtitle/author/date/status/abstract/keywords; contents and figure/table lists; running headers and centered page numbers. Main text begins after front matter and finishes with the required two-sentence Xylia process credit immediately before the references.

**Section and math hierarchy:** numbered sections/subsections; theorem/lemma environments used only for formally stated claims; assumptions introduced before use; `cleveref` for cross-references.

**Tables:** booktabs, no vertical rules, deliberate column widths, six substantive tables: adjacent literature, notation, model assumptions, coverage guarantees, synthetic results, P1-P5 classification.

**Figures:** all vector PDF/SVG generated by `verify_model.py`; consistent DejaVu Sans chart labels and muted blue/teal/rose palette. Twelve numbered figures: eight analytic plots, two graph/network views, two workflow diagrams. Synthetic evidence clearly labeled.

**References:** manually typeset `thebibliography` because BibTeX was not installed; entries and citation keys cross-checked against `paper.bib`.

**Acceptance features:** contents, lists of figures/tables, early framework graphic, consistent running headers, result table with observed outputs, integrated appendices and references. No fabricated headline efficacy metric.
