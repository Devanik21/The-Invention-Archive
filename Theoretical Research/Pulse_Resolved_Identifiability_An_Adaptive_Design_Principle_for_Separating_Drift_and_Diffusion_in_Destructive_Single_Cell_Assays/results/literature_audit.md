# Literature audit — 2026-10-24

## Scope and novelty boundary
The review focused on (1) unpaired/destructive single-cell perturbation data, (2) trajectory and stochastic dynamics inferred from time-stamped snapshots, (3) Fisher-information and optimal design for stochastic systems, and (4) recent methods that might duplicate the proposed contribution. The search was targeted rather than exhaustive. The paper does not claim that optimal design, D-optimality, pulse system identification, or Fisher-information analysis is new.

## What is and is not novel here
The paper's exact ratio identity follows from the solution of a scalar controlled linear SDE; it is not presented as a new general theorem in system identification. The proposed synthesis is the explicit moment-level account of what is and is not identifiable in a destructive-snapshot pulse experiment, linked to a transparent local greedy design and a released adversarial synthetic benchmark. The closest prior work includes Fox et al. (2020) and Iolov et al. (2017), so the manuscript explicitly avoids a broad priority claim.

## Unresolved literature questions
A follow-up should compare the design against existing inference and trajectory methods on public perturbation time courses, assess donor-aware hierarchical models, and search for any more recent work specifically combining post-pulse moment ratios with adaptive destructive-sampling schedules. No real dataset was downloaded in this cycle.
