# Publication design brief

- Audience: computational biology, aging-biomarker, and experimental-design researchers.
- Venue style: restrained journal-style A4 preprint; no submission or acceptance is implied.
- Page system: A4, 0.82-inch margins, 10 pt NewTX text/math, 1.06 line spacing, running header and centered page numbers.
- Hierarchy: title/subtitle, abstract, scope callout, contents, numbered sections, appendices, references.
- Equations: aligned displays with prose definitions and explicit assumptions.
- Tables: booktabs rules, concise captions, consistent notation, no vertical rules.
- Figures: vector PDF/SVG, common typography, navy/teal/gold/coral palette, captions distinguish synthetic and conceptual material.
- Release gate: compile current source, inspect every rendered page, fix critical/major defects, and bind review to the final PDF hash.
