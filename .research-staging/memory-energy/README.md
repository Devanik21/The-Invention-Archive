# The Memory--Energy Frontier in Noisy Recurrent Inference

**A Spectral Rate--Distortion Framework for Neural and Neuromorphic Systems**

**Author:** Devanik Debnath  
**Affiliation:** Electronics and Communication Engineering, National Institute of Technology Agartala, India  
**Release date:** 2026-10-24

## Research question

When a recurrent estimator must operate under a declared activity budget, how much estimation error is paid for a lower-cost gain, and can a learned policy approximate the constrained optimum without violating that budget?

The paper derives an exact stationary MSE for a scalar linear recurrence, defines a dimensionless activity proxy, trains a small MLP to approximate a finite-grid oracle, and applies a deterministic feasibility projection. The work is deliberately scoped: it is not a new Kalman filter, a physical energy law, or empirical evidence about neural metabolism or hardware efficiency.

## Core equations

The latent state and noisy observation are

$$
x_t = a x_{t-1} + w_t,\qquad |a|<1
$$

$$
y_t = x_t + v_t
$$

with independent white Gaussian noises of variances $q>0$ and $r>0$. The estimator is

$$
\widehat{x}_t = a(1-k)\widehat{x}_{t-1} + k y_t,\qquad k\in[0,1].
$$

Its stationary mean-squared error is

$$
P(k)=\frac{(1-k)^2q+k^2r}{1-a^2(1-k)^2}.
$$

The declared activity proxy is

$$
C(k)=\alpha k^2+\beta a^2(1-k)^2,\qquad \alpha,\beta>0.
$$

For a proxy budget $B$, the constrained gain is selected from

$$
\mathcal{K}_B=\{k\in[0,1]:C(k)\leq B\}.
$$

The proxy is dimensionless unless calibrated. It is not ATP, joules, or a device-level energy model.

## Reproducibility

The released script generates all data-bearing plots, source CSVs, policy outputs, environment information, and numerical assertions from a fixed seed. No external data or network access is required by the model script.

~~~bash
python -m pip install numpy==2.3.5 matplotlib==3.10.8 scikit-learn==1.8.0 networkx==3.6.1 PyMuPDF==1.26.7 PyYAML==6.0.3 Pillow==11.3.0
python verify_model.py
python deploy.py --self-test
python deploy.py --build
python deploy.py --validate-only
~~~

The build requires a working LaTeX toolchain with pdflatex and bibtex, plus Poppler utilities for PDF preflight. The script writes actual numerical outputs to results/, and the paper imports generated metric macros from results/metrics.tex.

## Prior work and novelty boundary

The relevant foundations include Kalman filtering, recurrent associative memory, energy-aware estimation, experimental estimates of neural information cost, Landauer's principle, and modern neuromorphic hardware reviews. Energy-constrained Kalman filtering is established prior art. The scoped distinction here is the reproducible combination of a scalar closed-form MSE, an explicit internal activity proxy, a learned gain surrogate with hard feasibility projection, and a shifted synthetic evaluation. No absolute priority claim is made.

## Limitations and next validation step

The model is scalar and linear; the gain oracle is optimal only over the declared grid; the OOD generator is limited; and the activity proxy is not physically calibrated. The next defensible step is to implement the recurrence on a named device, measure energy per update and task error, and test whether the proxy predicts measured quantities out of sample. A biological study would require a defined preparation, appropriate ethics/approvals, and independent energetic and functional measures.
